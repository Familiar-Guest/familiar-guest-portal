# Familiar Guest

<div class="subtitle">Direct booking for your rental — without the hassle</div>

You already have guests who love your place. They come back year after year, they refer their friends, and they trust you. So why are you still paying Airbnb 15% every time they book?

**Familiar Guest** lets you take bookings directly from your own guests — with payments, rental agreements, and trust protections handled automatically. No website to build. No software to learn. Just share a link.

---

## How It Works

**You're live in under an afternoon.** Seriously.

1. **Set up your listing** — upload a few photos, add your description (or let AI write one for you), and connect your Airbnb calendar so availability stays in sync.

2. **Get verified once** — a quick identity check (takes most people under five minutes) so your guests know they're booking with a real, verified owner.

3. **Share your booking link** — text it, email it, put it in your holiday card. Your guest sees your property name, your photos, your price. Not ours.

4. **We handle the rest** — payment processing, a digital rental agreement, automated check-in instructions, and a house manual your guests can pull up on their phone.

<div class="callout">
<p><strong>Your guests never create an account.</strong> They click your link, pick their dates, pay, sign the rental agreement, and they're confirmed. Works beautifully on mobile.</p>
</div>

## Why This Beats What You're Doing Now

### vs. Airbnb (for guests you already know)
- **Keep 100% of your rate** instead of giving up 15.5% per booking
- **Own the guest relationship** — their contact info, stay history, and preferences belong to you
- **No platform ranking games** — your repeat guests book directly with you

### vs. Venmo / "just send me the money"
- **Escrow protection** — guest funds are held until check-in, so both sides are protected
- **Rental agreement included** — auto-generated, digitally signed, stored forever
- **Looks professional** — a branded booking page, not a Venmo request

### vs. Building your own website
- **No website to maintain.** No hosting, no plugins, no updates.
- **Payments, agreements, and messaging built in** — you don't assemble anything
- **Calendar syncs with Airbnb, VRBO, and others** so you never double-book

---

## What Your Guests See

When you share your booking link, your guest lands on a clean, mobile-friendly page that shows:

- Your property name and photos (not our brand — yours)
- Available dates (synced from your calendar)
- Price breakdown with no hidden fees
- A "Verified Owner" badge
- A digital rental agreement to sign before paying
- Secure payment via credit card

After booking, they automatically get confirmation, check-in instructions, and a digital house manual — all on your schedule, all customizable.

---

## Built-In Trust (for You and Your Guests)

This is the part that makes direct booking actually work for both sides:

| Protection | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Escrow** | Guest payment is held until check-in — not released early. If something goes wrong, the money hasn't disappeared. |
| **Verified Owner** | Every owner passes an identity check. Your guests know you're real. |
| **Rental Agreement** | Auto-generated per booking, digitally signed, legally stored. No more PDF email chains. |
| **Damage Deposit** | Held separately, released automatically after your inspection window. |
| **Guest Screening** | Optional ID and fraud check for guests you don't know yet. |
